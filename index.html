Explore the melodic modes

These traditional mode names offer a starting point for learning melodic movement in recitation. Instruction is guided by Tajweed, measured delivery and the teacher’s feedback.

  • BayatiWarm, gentle movement
  • RastBalanced, steady tone
  • HijazDistinctive, expressive color
  • NahawandReflective movement
  • SabaDeep, plaintive color
  • SikahIntimate, tender movement
  • JiharkahBright, expansive tone
  • KurdCalm, direct movement

Questions learners ask

Before your first lesson

Can I start if I am a complete beginner?

Yes. Share your current reading level when you get in touch. Lessons can begin with letter recognition and Qaida foundations or focus on an existing recitation goal.

What happens in the free trial lesson?

You can discuss your goals, read a short passage if you are comfortable, and learn what a suitable lesson plan could cover. Use the contact form to request a time.

Do I need to know Tajweed before studying Maqamat?

No prior Maqamat study is required. The teaching approach emphasizes sound pronunciation and Tajweed foundations as you explore melodic recitation.

Are lessons available for children and adults?

Lessons can be discussed for different ages and levels. Include the learner’s age and preferred schedule in your inquiry so the teacher can respond with a suitable option.
